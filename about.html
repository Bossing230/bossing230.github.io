Hi, I'm Joseph.

Computer Science Student · Aspiring Full-Stack Developer · UI/UX Enthusiast

I'm an Computer Science student who genuinely enjoys the process of turning a blank screen into something people can actually use — a form that validates itself, a dashboard that finally makes sense of messy data, an app that feels quick and calm to use. That satisfaction is what pulled me from "interested in computers" to building software on purpose.

My education has been hands-on from the start: coursework in databases, programming fundamentals, and software engineering, paired with self-driven projects where I actually ship something at the end. I like learning by building, not just reading about it.

Looking ahead, my goal is to grow into a full-stack developer who's equally comfortable designing a clean interface and structuring the database behind it — and eventually contribute to products used by real people, not just graded by a rubric.

Outside of coding, I'm into UI/UX rabbit holes, sketching layout ideas before I ever open an editor, and the occasional late-night refactor that "will only take five minutes."

Consistent

Small, steady progress beats last-minute sprints. I build in increments.

Detail-driven

The last 10% — spacing, states, edge cases — is where quality actually lives.

Curious

I'd rather learn a new tool by breaking it in a sandbox than avoid it.
